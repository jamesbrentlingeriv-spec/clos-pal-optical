Chapter 1: Welcome to C.L.O.S. & System Overview

The Contact Lens Ordering System (C.L.O.S.) was engineered specifically for Pal Optical to replace messy physical paper tracking with a lightning-fast, multi-computer digital system that exactly mirrors our real-world office paperwork.

Why C.L.O.S. Exists

In our optical practice, contact lens orders move through three distinct phases across multiple staff members:

1
Phase 1: Front Desk Intake (The 16-Row Daily Pad)

When patients call or visit to order contacts, the front desk collects their name, birthdate, phone number, box quantities, and insurance allowance notes. These accumulate throughout the day on the 16-row pad.

2
Phase 2: Doctor Prescription & Ordering (The Doctor Queue)

Dr. Robbins reviews the orders on her computer, enters the verified contact lens prescription (OD/OS power, cylinder, axis, base curve, diameter, and brand), places the order with the supplier, and sends the order forward.

3
Phase 3: Package Arrival & Patient Pickup (The 50-Row Waiting Sheet)

When boxes arrive from distributors, they are checked in, placed in numbered drawers (1–12), and patients are called. When the patient arrives, the front desk checks them out, records how they paid, and logs the pickup date.

Key Benefits Over Old Paper Sheets

  • Zero Lost Orders: Everything is stored safely in the database and saved automatically every second.
  • Multi-Computer Synchronization: Front desk computers and Dr. Robbins' station communicate seamlessly across the office local network in real-time.
  • Exact Paper Replicas: If you ever need physical paper, the Print button creates an exact 1-to-1 landscape replica of our original 16-row order pad and 50-row tracking sheet.
  • Instant Search: Find any patient by name, phone number, brand, or drawer number in under 1 second.

Chapter 3: Daily Contact Lens Orders Pad (16 Rows)

The Daily Orders Pad tab (📋) is where front desk staff record incoming contact lens orders throughout the day. It matches the physical 16-row paper pad used at Pal Optical.

How to Add a Patient in 15 Seconds (Quick-Add Bar)

Instead of manually typing into small table cells, use the Quick Add Patient Order dock at the top of the screen:

1
Enter Patient Information

Type the patient's name into Patient Name. Enter their birthdate into DOB (the system automatically formats slashes like 04/15/1985). Type their phone number into Phone (automatically formats as (859) 555-0199).

2
Click Quantity Pre-Fill Chips

Click one of the pre-set box chips to instantly fill the quantity field: 1 Box 1 Box Each Six Month Supply Annual Supply. You can also change the number spinner (e.g. 2) and click "2 Boxes Each".

3
Select Billing / Insurance Type

Click the patient's insurance or billing method: Self Pay Eyemed VSP Spectera Versant Premier Vision. It immediately appends the billing label to the instructions column.

4
Special Instructions & Insurance Allowance Calculator

Check the box "Add Special Instructions / Allowance Details for Doctor" to open quick allowance presets: "Only $50 allowance left from original $250" or "Dr. Robbins trial fit only". You can also type custom dollar amounts.

5
Click "+ Add to Pad"

Click the blue + Add to Pad button. The patient is instantly placed into the next empty row on the 16-row pad, and your inputs clear ready for the next patient.

Individual Row Actions

Each row on the daily pad has helpful action buttons in the rightmost column (omitted during printing):

  • 🗑️ Clear Row: Erases that specific row with one click. If the row was already sent to the Doctor's Queue, C.L.O.S. will ask if you also want to remove it from the doctor's station.
  • ✏️ Edit Order: If the row has been processed, clicking the pencil lets you view and edit full order details.
  • Direct Cell Typing: You can also click directly into any cell on the 16 rows and type directly, just like on paper.

Sending Orders to Dr. Robbins

When you have entered orders and are ready for Dr. Robbins to write the prescription and place the order with distributors, click the green button:

🩺 Send Batch to Doctor (X Orders)

All filled rows are instantly transmitted across the office network to Dr. Robbins' screen.

Chapter 4: Doctor's Ordering Station & Rx Queue

The Doctor's Ordering Queue tab (🩺) is Dr. Robbins' command center. When the front desk enters orders, they appear here organized as individual cards awaiting clinical review and supplier ordering.

Step-by-Step: How Dr. Robbins Orders Contacts

1
Review the Order Card

Each card displays the patient's name, birthdate, phone number, box quantities requested, and any front-desk insurance allowance notes (e.g. "VSP: Only $130 allowance left").

2
Click "Order Contacts & Enter Rx"

Clicking the blue button opens the Doctor Prescription & Order Entry Modal.

3
Select or Type the Contact Lens Brand

Click any quick-brand chip to fill the brand instantly: Biofinity Multifocals (trials) Air Optix Astig Oasys Multi Ultra Astig Acuvue 2 Dailies Total 1, or type any custom brand name.

4
Enter Right (OD) & Left (OS) Prescriptions

Type the Sphere, Cylinder, Axis, Add, or Base Curve into the Rx (Right) and Rx (Left) fields.
Time-Saver Tip: If both eyes share the same prescription, click the ⇄ Copy Right Eye to Left Eye button to duplicate the parameters in one click.

5
Add Dispensing / Fitting Notes

Select quick doctor notes that print directly under the patient's name on office sheets: "CAN TAKE AND TRY" *NEEDS FITTING. WILL OWE $50* DONT CALL *FIT SCHEDULED*.

6
Set Amount Due & Paid Status

Click trials (for complimentary diagnostic pairs) or click pre-set amounts like $130.00 or $220.00.

7
Click "✅ Mark as Ordered & Send to Waiting Sheet"

Clicking the green button submits the order. The system marks it as ordered with today's timestamp, removes it from the doctor's pending queue, and automatically adds the patient to the 50-row Waiting to be Picked Up Sheet.

The Doctor Security Rule

To protect clinical accuracy and medical prescriptions, C.L.O.S. enforces a strict separation of permissions:

🟢
1. WHAT FRONT DESK STAFF CAN DO FREELY (NO PASSWORD):
• How They Paid: Type directly into the Pd? column or change payment method anytime.
• Date They Picked Up: Click ✓ Done on the sheet or ✅ Picked Up in the drawer rack to check out the patient and record the pickup date.
🔒
2. WHAT REQUIRES THE DOCTOR'S PASSWORD (pal):
• Clicking ✏️ Edit Order to change patient name, prescriptions, brands, quantities, or notes.
• Clicking 🗑️ Delete Order to permanently delete an order from the database.
• Manually changing arrival dates, assigning drawers, or modifying phone call logs.
• Clicking Clear All Orders to empty the waiting sheet.

Chapter 6: Patient Pickup & Checkout Workflow

When a patient walks into Pal Optical to pick up their contact lenses, any front desk staff member can complete their checkout in under 10 seconds without needing any password.

Step-by-Step Checkout Guide for Front Desk

1
Find the Patient on the 50-Row Sheet or Drawer Rack

Scan down the Waiting to be Picked Up sheet or open the 🗄️ Drawer Rack. Check the Drawer # column to know which drawer their lenses are sitting in.

2
Click the "✓ Done" Button

Click the green ✓ Done button on their row (or click ✅ Picked Up inside the Drawer Rack). The Patient Contact Pickup Modal will instantly appear on your screen.

3
Confirm Date Picked Up

The Date Patient Picked Up field automatically fills with today's date (e.g. 10/25/2026). If the patient picked up yesterday, you can change this date.

4
Confirm How They Paid

The modal shows the patient's payment status. Click one of the quick payment chips: Paid Cash Card Check VSP Eyemed Self Pay.

5
Click "✅ Confirm Pickup & Archive"

Clicking the green confirmation button completes the checkout:
• The patient is removed from the active 50-row Waiting Sheet.
• Their drawer is marked empty and ready for the next patient.
• The order is permanently archived in Search Past Order History for future reference.

Chapter 10: Multi-Computer Sync, Backups & FAQ

How Office Synchronization Works

C.L.O.S. uses a hybrid synchronization engine designed for doctor offices:

  • Instant Cross-Tab Sync: If two browser tabs or windows are open on the same computer, changes sync across them in milliseconds.
  • Multi-Computer LAN Sync: When the background server is active, computers in Dr. Robbins' room and the front desk automatically sync over the local network every 10 seconds.
  • Sync Status Dot: The green pulse dot in the navigation bar indicates when your screen is synchronized with the office network.

Data Backups & Excel Export

At the top right of the executive header, three powerful data tools are available:

Button What It Does When to Use It
💾 Backup Downloads a complete JSON backup file (pal-clos-backup-[date].json). Recommended once a week or before major office holidays.
📊 CSV Exports all orders as an Excel-compatible spreadsheet (.csv). Use for office inventory auditing or year-end financial reporting.
📥 Restore Lets you select a previously downloaded backup file to restore all orders. Use if a computer was replaced or data was accidentally erased.

Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Q1
"I made a typo in a patient's name on the Waiting Sheet. How do I fix it?"

Click the ✏️ Edit button on their row. The system will prompt for the Doctor's Password (pal). Type pal, click Authorize, and the full Order Modal will open where you can fix the name, phone number, prescription, or any other field. Click 💾 Save Order Changes when done.

Q2
"A patient picked up their contacts. Do I need the doctor's password to check them out?"

No! Front desk staff can check out patients without any password. Simply click the green ✓ Done button on their row (or ✅ Picked Up in the Drawer Rack), verify the date, choose how they paid (e.g. Paid, Cash, VSP), and click Confirm.

Q3
"How do I scroll down the 50-row Waiting Sheet on a laptop?"

Use your mouse scroll wheel or trackpad swipe. As you scroll down through rows 1 to 50, the column headers stick permanently to the top of the screen so you always know which column is Date, Patient, Phone, Brand, Rx, Drawer, or Called.

Q4
"What happens if our computer loses internet connection?"

C.L.O.S. runs locally on our office computer network and is configured as a Progressive Web App (PWA). It functions completely offline and saves all data directly to your local computer drive without needing external internet access.

Q5
"What is the Doctor's Password if the doctor is not at the desk?"

The Doctor's Security Password is pal (all lowercase, no spaces).
