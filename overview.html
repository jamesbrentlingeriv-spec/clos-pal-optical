Drawer Rack & Pickup

Front desk staff verifies patient arrival, opens the visual 19-drawer rack, logs payment, and completes pickup in 1 click.

Enterprise Capabilities

Engineered Specifically for Pal Optical

Every screen is designed around your physical clinic forms and real daily workflow, offering seamless transition and zero learning curve.

📋

16-Row Daily Order Pad

1-to-1 pixel-perfect recreation of Pal Optical's physical 16-row contact lens order pad.

  • Fast keyboard Tab flow between fields
  • Quick billing chips (VSP, EyeMed, Spectera, Self Pay)
  • Automated allowance calculations ($130 / $220 / $280)
  • 1-click landscape printing (filled or 100% blank)
🩺

Doctor Station & Rx Builder

Optometrist prescription suite supporting all contact lens modalities with zero manual math.

  • Toric Contacts: Full Cylinder & Axis (° with chips)
  • Multifocal Contacts: Add Power with Low, Medium, High chips
  • Toric Multifocals: Both Cyl/Axis and Add power
  • Fit Specs: Base Curve (8.4–8.8) & Diameter (14.0–14.5)
  • ⇄ 1-click OD to OS prescription duplication
🛡️

Insurance Carrier & Coverage

Comprehensive tracking of managed vision care plans and copay arrangements.

  • Carrier naming (VSP, EyeMed, Spectera, Davis, Humana)
  • Insurance Full Amount vs Insurance Partial
  • Auto-formats clean status onto physical pickup sheets
  • Separate staff and doctor privilege authorization
📦

50-Row Waiting to Pick Up

Exact replica of the front desk 50-row physical tracking ledger (11" x 8.5" landscape format).

  • 50 full rows for high-volume practice tracking
  • Date Received, Assigned Drawer #, Date Called / LVM
  • Unblocked top toolbar with maximum input visibility
  • Print filled waiting sheet or blank pads anytime
🗄️

19 Visual Cabinet Drawers

Color-coded live inventory grid representing physical office storage drawers 1 through 19.

  • Supports all 19 office drawers + Unassigned tray
  • Live count of boxes and patients stored in each drawer
  • Click any drawer to view patient cards and dispense
  • Quick search drawer lookup from any screen
🔄

Multi-Computer Live Sync

Links front desk workstations and doctor exam lanes seamlessly across the local network and cloud.

  • Local-network sync runs even if internet is down
  • Encrypted cloud backups with 1-click JSON & Excel CSV export
  • PWA installed offline application support
  • Secure doctor password protection (eye_doctor)
